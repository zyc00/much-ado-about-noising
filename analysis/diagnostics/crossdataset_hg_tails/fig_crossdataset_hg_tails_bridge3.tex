  \caption{\textbf{Residual tails remain heavier than Gaussian after HG scaling.}
  We probe pure heteroscedastic-Gaussian policies on 24 RoboCasa-GR1 tasks
  and three Bridge instruction groups. Residuals are divided by the policy's
  predicted scale, then centered and standardized per task and action-chunk
  coordinate using episode-level cross-fitting.
  \textbf{(a)} Each point represents one task's frequency of $|z|>3$, divided
  by the standard Gaussian reference ($0.27\%$); horizontal intervals are
  95\% episode-bootstrap intervals conditional on the fitted calibration.
  Tasks are ordered by this ratio.
  \textbf{(b)} Empirical two-sided tail probabilities, with the Gaussian
  reference dashed. All tasks are shown; colors highlight the tasks nearest
  the 10th, 50th, and 90th percentiles of the ratio in (a), with matching
  colors across columns. All three Bridge groups are highlighted.
  GR1 hand joints are retained, while Bridge's binary gripper is excluded.
  Episode holdout applies to calibration, not policy training.}
  \label{fig:crossdataset-hg-tails}
