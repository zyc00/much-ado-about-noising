\section*{Residual-scale diagnostic}
\input{figure}
Residual magnitudes span multiple scales across demonstration inputs
(Figure~\ref{fig:multiscale-rms}). After removing differences in median
scale between tasks, the 10th--90th percentile range of MSE residual RMS
spans 4.6$\times$ on GR1, 2.1$\times$ on LIBERO, 1.9$\times$ on Tool-Hang,
and 1.8$\times$ on Transport. In a separate GR1 HT probe, the learned
input-dependent scale is strongly associated with the measured residual
RMS (Spearman $\rho=0.91$). These fitting diagnostics motivate an
input-dependent scale in the regression objective.
\paragraph{Scope of this diagnostic.}
The measurements describe fitting residuals, not isolated demonstration
noise. The two panels use different sampled inputs and are not a paired
MSE--HT performance comparison. The observed spread does not establish
discrete Gaussian components, and the HT panel is a training-set check,
not held-out calibration.
