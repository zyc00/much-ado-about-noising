# RoboMimic: five heads x three backbones (our harness, one protocol)

Cell = **best checkpoint / late-checkpoint average**. All 30 rows are our own runs under a
single eval protocol, so unlike `main_table.md` (which compares our HT against DP and MIP
numbers quoted from their papers) every comparison here is protocol-matched.

Heads: **Flow** = flow matching; **Regression** = plain MSE single-pass; **Straight Flow** =
one-step/straightened flow; **MIP** = minimum iterative policy (2 steps); **HT (ours)** =
single-pass heteroscedastic Student-t.

Column order and the state/image assignment are as supplied. NOTE, UNVERIFIED: in these
runs the mh column is above its ph counterpart on square and transport in essentially every
row, which is the reverse of the published DP/MIP tables; if the ph/mh labels are swapped the
affected columns are 5-8. The `(c)` and `*` markers on the MIP cells were dropped as instructed.
