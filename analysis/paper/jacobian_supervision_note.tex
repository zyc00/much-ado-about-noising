\paragraph{Notation.} Observation $x \in \mathbb{R}^d$ (normalized window),
action chunk $y \in \mathbb{R}^m$. Expert conditional $p(y|x)$ with mean
$\mu(x)$; on clean data $p(y|x)=\delta(y-\mu(x))$. Network $f_\theta$; encoder
Jacobian $J_x = \partial f/\partial x$. Data measure $\rho(x)$ over a
\emph{finite} set of windows. ``Stiffness'' means the curvature of the
population loss at its optimum --- the restoring force resisting parameter
drift; its relevance comes from the SGD stationary-fluctuation relation
(\S\ref{sec:ou}), which converts (stiffness, gradient noise) into (retained,
eroded).

\section{MSE: the Jacobian is only supervised through finite differences}

\begin{equation}
L_{\mathrm{MSE}} = \mathbb{E}_{x,y}\,\|f(x)-y\|^2, \qquad f^{*}=\mu .
\end{equation}

\subsection{Value-space curvature contains no derivative term \hfill \tagd}
Perturb $f \to f^{*} + \varepsilon g$:
\begin{equation}
\Delta L = \varepsilon^{2}\,\mathbb{E}_{\rho}\,\|g(x)\|^{2}.
\end{equation}
Curvature in direction $g$ depends \emph{only on the values of $g$ at the data
points}. A perturbation vanishing on the $N$ data points but with a large
gradient between them costs exactly zero population loss: with finite data,
such perturbations form an infinite-dimensional flat manifold, and nothing in
the objective selects among Jacobians consistent with the values.

\subsection{The implicit stiffness on $J_x$ \hfill \tagd}
The Jacobian is pinned only through \emph{pairs} of nearby samples. Take $x$
and a neighbor $x' = x+\delta v$ ($\|v\|=1$) with labels $\mu(x),\mu(x')$.
Fitting both forces the directional derivative
$J_x v \approx (\mu(x')-\mu(x))/\delta$. If the network carries $J_x v = D$
instead, the value error at $x'$ is $\delta\,\|D-\partial_v\mu\|+O(\delta^2)$,
so aggregating neighbors, the quadratic form on the Jacobian error
$E = J_x - \partial\mu/\partial x$ is
\begin{equation}
\Delta L \;\approx\; \mathbb{E}_{x'\sim\rho_{\mathrm{near}\,x}}
\big\|E\,(x'-x)\big\|^{2}
\;=\;\operatorname{tr}\!\big(E\,\Sigma_\rho(x)\,E^{\top}\big),
\label{eq:msestiff}
\end{equation}
with $\Sigma_\rho(x)$ the \emph{local input covariance of the data} around
$x$. Consequences: (i) the \emph{target} for $J_x$ is $\partial\mu/\partial x$
--- in a label-poor region ($\mu$ locally constant) MSE \emph{demands} a
degenerate Jacobian; (ii) the \emph{stiffness} is $\Sigma_\rho$-weighted:
directions the data does not locally vary in, or regions rarely visited, get
near-zero restoring curvature. With label noise $\sigma_n^{2}$ on the pair,
the finite-difference estimate of $\partial_v\mu$ has variance
$\sigma_n^{2}/\delta^{2}$: \textbf{the gradient signal on $J_x$ has SNR
$\delta\,|\partial_v\mu|/\sigma_n$} --- small label variation under noise
makes the Jacobian channel signal-free.

\subsection{Erosion corollary \hfill \tagd{} + \tagm}
\label{sec:ou}
Model SGD along a curvature eigendirection with stiffness $\kappa$, per-step
gradient-noise power $s^{2}$, learning rate $\eta$ (Ornstein--Uhlenbeck):
\begin{equation}
\operatorname{Var}_{\infty}(\theta) \;\approx\; \frac{\eta\,s^{2}}{2\kappa}.
\label{eq:ou}
\end{equation}
For MSE at convergence on data containing an irreducible residual mass
(aliased switch timing / aleatoric noise), $s^{2}=O(1)$ \emph{forever}, while
$\kappa$ on fine distinctions is the tiny $\Sigma_\rho$-weighted quantity of
Eq.~\eqref{eq:msestiff}. The stationary excursion exceeds the distinction
scale $\Rightarrow$ erased; the surviving Jacobian concentrates on the few
directions with large label-variation stiffness. Measured: settle-metric PR
$13.4\!\to\!2.0$ with $s_{\max}/s_{\mathrm{med}}\!\to\!61$ on pointing data;
absent on MP data ($s^{2}\!\to\!0$: PR arrests at $5.1$); reproduced with
visual controls in the erosion toy.

\section{Flow matching / MIP / score-based: the Jacobian is an explicit
target}

\subsection{Conditional targets and their Jacobians \hfill \tagd}
Interpolant $y_t = (1-t)\epsilon + t y$, $\epsilon\sim\mathcal N(0,I_m)$,
$t\sim U(0,1)$. For the deterministic conditional $y=\mu(x)$, given
$(x,y_t,t)$ one can invert $\epsilon = (y_t - t\mu)/(1-t)$, so every standard
regression target is a \emph{closed-form function of} $(x,y_t,t)$:
\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{2.0}
\begin{tabular}{llll}
\toprule
parameterization & target & $\partial(\cdot)/\partial y_t$ &
$\partial(\cdot)/\partial x$ \\
\midrule
velocity ($v=y-\epsilon$) & $v^{*}=\dfrac{\mu(x)-y_t}{1-t}$ &
$-\dfrac{1}{1-t}I_m$ & $\dfrac{1}{1-t}\dfrac{\partial\mu}{\partial x}$ \\
$\epsilon$-prediction & $\epsilon^{*}=\dfrac{y_t - t\mu(x)}{1-t}$ &
$\dfrac{1}{1-t}I_m$ & $-\dfrac{t}{1-t}\dfrac{\partial\mu}{\partial x}$ \\
score $\nabla_{y_t}\log p_t$ & $s^{*}=-\dfrac{y_t-t\mu(x)}{(1-t)^{2}}$ &
$-\dfrac{1}{(1-t)^{2}}I_m$ &
$\dfrac{t}{(1-t)^{2}}\dfrac{\partial\mu}{\partial x}$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Two structural facts shared by all three parameterizations:

\textbf{(A) A full-rank isotropic Jacobian block is explicitly demanded.}
The target's $y_t$-derivative is $\pm I_m/(1-t)^{k}$ --- rank $m$, isotropic,
at \emph{every} $(x,t)$. Unlike \S1.1 this is not an implicit
finite-difference constraint: the loss input itself sweeps a full-dimensional
Gaussian ball of $y_t$ around $t\mu(x)$ (radius $1-t$), so value constraints
on the ball pin the derivative \emph{within} it by direct integration --- the
network must hold $m$ independent unit-scale (after the $(1-t)$ factor)
sensitivity directions everywhere on the tube. Toy verification:
$(1-t)\,\partial v/\partial y_t = -1.02\pm0.25$ vs.\ the analytic $-1$ \tagm.

\textbf{(B) The $x$-Jacobian is supervised with amplification
$t/(1-t)^{k}$.} A network error $\delta\mu(x)$ in the transmitted
state-dependence costs $\|\delta\mu\|^{2}/(1-t)^{2}$ at time $t$ (velocity
form), so integrated over $t\sim U(0,1)$, the stiffness on every
state-discriminating direction that $\mu$ uses is
\begin{equation}
\kappa_{\mathrm{FM}}(x,v) \;\propto\;
\Big(\int_0^{1}\!\frac{dt}{(1-t)^{2}}\Big)\,\rho(x)\,\|\partial_v\mu\|^{2}
\;\gg\; \kappa_{\mathrm{MSE}}(x,v),
\end{equation}
the integral cut off at the smallest retained noise scale (or by time
discretization). Note the scope: the stiffness \emph{per unit of true label
variation} is amplified; a direction with $\partial_v\mu=0$ still has target
zero. Flow does not invent rank the task lacks --- it prevents the
\emph{pruning} of rank the task has, by making maintenance curvature large
relative to SGD noise (Eq.~\eqref{eq:ou} with boosted $\kappa$).

\subsection{Supervision over a measure: no memorization fixed point
\hfill \tagd{} + \tagm}
MSE's supervision set is the finite list $\{(x_i,\mu_i)\}$: memorizing it
gives zero loss and zero gradient --- from then on the noise of
\S\ref{sec:ou} is unopposed. FM's supervision set is
$\{(x_i, y_t, t)\}$ with fresh $(\epsilon,t)$ each step: a \emph{continuous
measure}. No finite parameter vector reaches zero loss on unseen draws unless
the function is correct as a function on the tube; the maintaining gradient
never vanishes. Experimental form: the frozen-noise control --- freezing
$(\epsilon_i,t_i)$ per sample (identical objective, finite list) restored
memorize-then-drift dynamics and destroyed the readout, while fresh draws held
fine structure indefinitely \tagm.

\subsection{Predictions vs.\ what stays empirical}
(i) Encoder-rank lift with a dataset-independent signature (the stiffness in
2.1B is objective-supplied) \tagd$\to$\tagm: MIP PR $\approx 5$--$7$, $k_{90}$
$2\times$ L2's, anisotropy halved, on pointing, MP-200, MP-2k, and human
data. (ii) Arrest level tracks the objective, not demo count \tagm: MIP
$4.2/4.8/5.2$ across $200/2\mathrm{k}/20\mathrm{k}$. (iii) \textbf{Off-support
behavior is not derivable from the objective}: the tube supervises
action-space perturbations at on-support $x$; state-space extrapolation
inherits only the trunk's smoothness bias. The restoring annulus field
($\overline{\Delta d}<0$), excursion capping ($5\%$ vs.\ $28\%$ escalation),
and off-manifold $\|J_x\|$ suppression (edgejac $1.4$--$2.6\times$) are
measured, consistent with but not implied by \S2.1 \tagm/\tago.

\section{Hetero-Gaussian NLL: the same implicit channel, re-priced}

\begin{equation}
L_{\mathrm{HG}} = \mathbb{E}\Big[\frac{\|f(x)-y\|^{2}}{2\sigma(x)^{2}}
 + m\log\sigma(x)\Big].
\end{equation}

\subsection{$\sigma$-stationarity \hfill \tagd}
At the optimum in $\sigma$ (holding $f$):
$\sigma^{2}(x) = \frac1m\,\mathbb{E}\big[\|y-f(x)\|^{2}\,\big|\,x\big]$ ---
the local mean-square residual. Substituting back, the gradient on $f$ is
$(y-f)/\sigma^{2}(x)$: \textbf{per-state equalized} --- every state
contributes $O(1)$ pressure regardless of residual scale.

\subsection{Jacobian stiffness: MSE's formula times $1/\sigma^{2}$
\hfill \tagd}
HG is a value-space loss, so Eq.~\eqref{eq:msestiff} applies with the weight
carried through:
\begin{equation}
\kappa_{\mathrm{HG}}(x,v) \;=\; \frac{\kappa_{\mathrm{MSE}}(x,v)}{\sigma^{2}(x)}.
\end{equation}
In the OU relation: in a fitted low-noise region
$\sigma^{2}\!\to\!\sigma_{\min}^{2}$ (floor), stiffness amplified up to
$1/\sigma_{\min}^{2}$; in a noisy region $\sigma^{2}\!\to$ the large
irreducible residual, so the gradient-noise power that region exports is
damped by $1/\sigma^{4}$ in variance. \textbf{Both numerator and denominator
of Eq.~\eqref{eq:ou} move favorably}, hence arrest-and-hold: scripted pointing
data, HG arrests at PR $6.5$ and holds $200$k$+$ steps while L2 slides to
$2.0$ \tagm; the constructive proof that weights alone carry it: \texttt{snorm}
($w = 1/(m+\sigma_{\min}^{2})$, plain weighted MSE, no likelihood) reaches SR
$93$ on true scripted data \tagm.

\subsection{Why HG's chart can exceed everyone's on noisy data
\hfill \tagd{} candidate + \tagm}
On heavy-tailed data the HG gradient $(y-f)/\sigma^{2}$ is \emph{unbounded in
$y$}: a tail draw at $10\sigma$ exerts $10\times$ force. Moving $f$ at $x_i$
without disturbing nearby $x_j$ requires a high-gain discriminating direction
(\S1.2 with $|\Delta y|$ large): outlier-chasing is \emph{rank-building
pressure}. Measured signature: HG's human chart is the richest of any arm
(PR $11.4$, $k_{90}$ $21$) while its $\mu$ placement is the most corrupted
(worst AS$=1$ cell, volatile bands) \tagm. The chart--corruption link is a
candidate, not derived; competing channel: the NLL can absorb an outlier by
inflating $\sigma(x)$ locally at only logarithmic cost --- which channel
dominates depends on the $\sigma$-head's smoothness \tago.

\section{Student-$t$ NLL: same amplification where fitted, bounded influence
where not}

\begin{equation}
L_{\mathrm{HT}} = \mathbb{E}\Big[\tfrac{\nu+m}{2}
\log\Big(1+\tfrac{\|y-f(x)\|^{2}}{\nu\sigma(x)^{2}}\Big)+m\log\sigma(x)\Big],
\qquad
\frac{\partial L}{\partial f} = -\,\underbrace{\frac{\nu+m}{\nu\sigma^{2}
+\|r\|^{2}}}_{w(r)}\,r,\quad r=y-f.
\end{equation}

\subsection{Two regimes of the effective weight \hfill \tagd}
\textbf{Small residuals} ($\|r\|^{2}\ll\nu\sigma^{2}$):
$w \to (\nu+m)/(\nu\sigma^{2})$ --- the same $1/\sigma^{2}$ re-pricing as HG.
On clean scripted data all residuals sit at the floor, so HT and HG are
gradient-identical up to a constant --- their metric ladders coincide to two
decimals ($6.46/6.47$) and both boundaries hold \tagm.
\textbf{Large residuals} ($\|r\|^{2}\gg\nu\sigma^{2}$):
$w \propto 1/\|r\|^{2}$, so the per-sample force $w\,r \propto r/\|r\|^{2}$
--- bounded influence, decaying in outlier size: the tail mass exerts
vanishing pressure on $f$, and therefore (by \S1.2) no rank-building pressure
either.

\subsection{Consequences by regime \hfill \tagd{} + \tagm}
Scripted side: HT $=$ HG (regime 1 everywhere); the tail is inert,
$\sigma$-repricing carries the arrest \tagm. Human side: HT declines to chase
the tail (regime 2 on heavy draws), so its chart stays L2-like (PR $5.2$ vs.\
HG $11.4$) while its $\mu$ is the robust location of the conditional (the
$t$-MLE), on the central mode instead of the tail-dragged mean. Its SR
dominance ($0.90$, flat over AS) with a plain spectrum is the strongest
evidence that on noisy data the operative quantity is $\mu$-placement, not
the Jacobian spectrum \tagm. Duality note: $w(r)$ is exactly the weight that
bounds the influence function of the location estimate --- loss tail
$\leftrightarrow$ noise tail (Student-$t$ NLL $=$ MLE under the measured
$t(2)$ demo noise).

\section{Summary}

\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.6}
\small
\begin{tabular}{p{2.4cm}p{5.6cm}p{3.2cm}p{3.2cm}}
\toprule
objective & stiffness $\kappa(x,v)$ on $J_x$ & supervision set & noise-mass
export $s^{2}$ \\
\midrule
MSE & $\rho(x)\,\lambda_{\Sigma_\rho}(v)$, target $\partial_v\mu$ &
finite list & $O(1)$, undamped \\
flow/MIP/score & $\times\!\int\!\frac{dt}{(1-t)^{2}}$ amplification $+$
explicit full-rank $y_t$-block $I_m/(1-t)^{k}$ & continuous measure (no
zero-loss fixed point) & absorbed by anchor/$\epsilon$-channel \\
hetero-G & $\kappa_{\mathrm{MSE}}/\sigma^{2}(x)$, $\sigma^{2}\!\to$ local
residual & finite list & damped $\times 1/\sigma^{4}$ \\
hetero-$t$ & $=$ HG for $\|r\|\ll\sqrt{\nu}\sigma$; $w\propto 1/\|r\|^{2}$
beyond & finite list & bounded per sample \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\textbf{Unifying statement.} Every objective that preserves the Jacobian does
so by keeping the maintenance curvature large relative to gradient noise in
the SGD stationary balance $\operatorname{Var}_\infty = \eta s^{2}/2\kappa$:
flow by amplifying $\kappa$ through the $1/(1-t)$ schedule \emph{and}
refreshing the supervision measure so the gradient never dies; the
hetero-NLLs by re-pricing $\kappa \to \kappa/\sigma^{2}$ \emph{and} damping
$s^{2}$ from the noise mass; MSE does neither, and its Jacobian survives only
where the data itself supplies curvature.

\textbf{Open items for derivation} (ranked): (1) off-support extension ---
why tube supervision at on-support $x$ yields the measured restoring annulus
field and $\|J_x\|$ suppression (candidate: the $\epsilon$-channel forces $f$
toward a contraction onto the action manifold in $y_t$; coupling to
$x$-extrapolation through the shared trunk's Lipschitz budget); (2) the HG
chart-building vs.\ $\sigma$-absorption competition (\S3.3); (3) the
human-data $10^{6}$ Frobenius concentration in L2/HT/MIP but not HG
(zero-variance input dims) \tagm, unexplained.
